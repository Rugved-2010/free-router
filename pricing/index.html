Usage and Rate Limits

Free Router sets no plan-based rate limit on paid models. A limit you hit there normally comes from the provider, and fallback routing retries other providers automatically; only very high burst traffic is rejected by Free Router's own DDoS protection with a 429. Free models are capped at 20 requests per minute and 50 per day, rising to 1,000 per day once your account has bought at least $10 of credits. Failed attempts count toward that daily cap, and a negative balance blocks free models until you add credits. Details are in the .
